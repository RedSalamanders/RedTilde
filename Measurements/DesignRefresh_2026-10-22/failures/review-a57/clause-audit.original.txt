Reviewed: 2026-10-23. Scope: every behavior, layout, theme, contextual-state, Files and link clause of `Specs/Plans/WIP/DesignRefresh_2026-10-22.md`, together with its accepted authoritative UI/Editor/Core rules. This is not covered by an appearance option. Root and the canonical owner were notified before final qualification. All remaining clauses were traced without another concrete app defect.
