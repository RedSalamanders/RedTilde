# Historical e62 application performance cohorts

This is a **derived public summary**, not a raw replay bundle. It records the pre-callback/network-repair RedTilde/DxUi state only; callback/network repair began after these collections. It does not qualify the repaired source or support a current-source performance conclusion.

[cohorts.json](cohorts.json) preserves the full 12-row metric comparison objects for AA and AB from each of two fixed 12-block primary studies (96 native slots per study), including every controlDrift array, finding, adjusted p-value, baseline/candidate median, source/pin/fixture/harness identity, collection interval, and independent-reference result. Each primary AB comparison records eight flagged rows; the AA and AB arms are separate, and the two 12-block studies are not pooled. The study records identify the same-binary control design. The packet retains unfavorable control results rather than filtering them.

The same JSON includes the separate four-block diagnostic and all 18 phase-review rows. It is not part of primary acceptance. Independent-reference counts are 7,988 for each primary study and 21,972 for the diagnostic; both referenced comparisons per study matched. The host summaries have complete sample coverage, but neither primary study nor the diagnostic established a quiet CPU window (`quietCpuWindowVerified: false`). Complete sampling does not establish a quiet or exclusive host, full process visibility, GPU attribution, or causal attribution.

The 22-entry `originalMetadataInventory` gives repository-relative source paths, byte lengths, and SHA256 values for the small collection, host summary, independent-reference, comparison, study, and diagnostic phase-review metadata. No absolute paths, raw activity/CI/foreground data, machine/desktop identifiers, native-report corpus, or binaries are included. The listed hashes are integrity references to privately retained source metadata; this summary cannot independently replay the raw measurements.

The reported eight AB flags and all same-binary control drift remain visible as recorded observations. Preserve them without pooling, recategorization, or a policy pass. These cohorts remain historical evidence for the e62 pin and are not evidence for any subsequent callback/network repair.
